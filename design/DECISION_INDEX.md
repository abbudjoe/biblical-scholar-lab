## Current DR-31 posture

DR-31 is additive. DR-01 through DR-30 remain approved historical authority. Training, learned-model serving or conversion, cloud-training, adapted-model hosting, `TNEVAL-A4`, and training/campaign-controller portions named by DR-31 are `DORMANT_PENDING_TRAINING_REACTIVATION_GATE`; this is a current program posture rather than deletion or broad supersession.

`TNEVAL-A0` is a future deterministic retrieval baseline requiring separate bounded design or activation and no provider. `TNEVAL-A1` through `TNEVAL-A3` are `UNSTARTED`, `NOT_AUTHORIZED`, `NOT_ON_CURRENT_CRITICAL_PATH`, `ELIGIBLE_FOR_SEPARATE_BOUNDED_API_EVALUATION_AUTHORITY`, and `NOT_GATED_BY_TRAINING_REACTIVATION`. They require separately frozen evaluation authority and applicable DR-22, DR-24, and DR-25 controls. `TNEVAL-A4` remains subject to every gate in DR-31 section 21.
